# Somatic — product register

Design **serves** the product. This is a local, consent-gated informational health **instrument**, not a marketing site and not an EHR.

**Audience:** one person on their own machine. Trust-first, privacy-first, accessibility-critical.

**Job:** make seven default-OFF capabilities feel calm and legible; never look like a clinic portal or a generic SaaS dashboard.

**Register:** product (impeccable `reference/product.md`). One family, fixed rem scale, state-complete components, 150–200ms motion, empty/loading/error as first-class.

**Mood phrase:** laboratory bench at dusk: white paper, moss ink, a seven-tick scope rail, no cream editorial serif.
